# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this repository to be a welcoming place for readers at every skill level who are working through the book. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for readers following the book

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the repository maintainer via a private GitHub issue or the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
